FAQ & Policies

Before you book

What is the difference between the two tickets?

The Standard Pass covers full-day access to the Main Stage and the Second Stage, with the basic Summaries, Action Pack and Report. The All Access Pass adds up to 5 workshops across the three workshop tracks, Exercises & Live Builds, Workshop Templates & Files, the full Summaries, Action Pack and Report, and 6 months of access to TrafficMENA's premium content library.

How do workshops work with the All Access Pass?

Workshops are offline and capacity-limited. Workshop booking opens 7-10 days before the event, and we will announce how to book. You can select up to 5 workshops; workshops at the same time cannot be selected together.

Do I get the Workshop Templates & Files?

Workshop Templates & Files are included with the All Access Pass only.

Can I upgrade from Standard to All Access later?

Yes, you can upgrade before the event. Workshop seats after upgrading are subject to availability.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

No. Tickets are non-transferable and cannot be reassigned to another attendee. For help, contact info@trafficmena.com or WhatsApp.

What is the refund policy?

A full refund is available until the end of 30 October 2026, five days before the event. To request one, email info@trafficmena.com from the email associated with your booking. Requests after this date cannot be accepted.

Can I get an invoice for my company?

Yes. An invoice can be requested during checkout or afterwards via info@trafficmena.com or WhatsApp.

Where is the venue?

ECommerce Day takes place at Creativa Innovation Hub, Giza, on 5 November 2026.

Is there a certificate?

No, there is no certificate. What you leave with is the Summaries, the Action Pack and the ECommerce Report, plus the Workshop Templates & Files with the All Access Pass.
