FAQ & Policies

Before you book

What is the difference between the two tickets?

The Control Tower Pass covers the Main Stage plus all recordings and Workshop Templates & Files. The Full Journey Pass adds entry to the three workshop tracks, workshop seat reservation, Exercises & Live Builds, the full Summaries, Action Pack and Report, and a full year of platform access.

How do workshops work with the Full Journey Pass?

Workshops are offline, capacity-limited and require pre-reservation after payment. Buying the pass gives you workshop access but does not automatically reserve a seat — reserve early.

Are session recordings included?

Yes. All sessions — Main Stage and workshops — are recorded and available on the platform with both tickets.

Do I get the Workshop Templates & Files?

Yes. Workshop Templates & Files are included with both tickets.

Can I upgrade from Control Tower to Full Journey later?

Yes, you can upgrade before the event. Workshop seats after upgrading are subject to availability.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Transfer rules are covered in the Ticket & Entry policy. For special cases contact info@trafficmena.com.

What is the refund policy?

Refunds and cancellations follow the Refund & Cancellation policy linked below. Review it before booking.

Can I get an invoice for my company?

Yes — an invoice can be requested during checkout or afterwards via info@trafficmena.com.

Where is the venue?

Venue details will be announced soon. All ticket holders are notified as soon as the venue is confirmed.

Is there a certificate?

No — there is no certificate. What you leave with is the recordings, the Workshop Templates & Files, the Summaries, the Action Pack and the ECommerce Report.
